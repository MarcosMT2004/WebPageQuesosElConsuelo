# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: buyers.** Private customers looking for artisan Manchego cheese for home or as a gift. They browse the ranges and place their order by WhatsApp or phone.
- **Secondary: visitors.** People who come to learn about the dairy (its history, how the cheese is made and the flock) without buying anything yet.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Quesos El Consuelo, an artisan dairy in Madridejos (Toledo). It presents the three cheese ranges and the dairy's story, and turns visits into a direct conversation by WhatsApp or phone. Success = more people get in touch to order.

The site is built by a family member of the owner, who reviews it in person. Every proposal has to work; a static mock-up is not enough.

## Positioning

- **Own flock:** the milk comes from the dairy's own Manchega sheep (S.A.T. Cañada Real Soriana). The dairy controls the whole cycle, from flock to cheese.
- **Award:** El Cordel DOP won Silver at the World Cheese Awards 2016.
- **Raw milk and DOP:** traditional cheesemaking with raw milk. The El Cordel range carries the Queso Manchego Protected Designation of Origin.
- **Direct, family relationship:** customers talk to the dairy itself, not to a shop.

## Operating Context

- Ordering goes through WhatsApp (+34 667 638 293) or a phone call. There is no online checkout.
- Ranges: **El Consuelo** (flagship line: fresco, semicurado, curado, in olive oil), **El Cordel** (DOP, award-winning), **El Redil** (most versatile range: aged sheep's cheese, goat's cheese with paprika, mild sheep's cheese).
- Other pages: company, cheesemaking, FAQ, contact, legal notice, privacy.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The stack is already set: Next.js 16, React 19, Tailwind 4, motion, shadcn/ui.
- Online shop: **on hold** until the client provides products, prices and payment method.
- Ideas waiting for approval are tracked in `PROPUESTAS.md`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photos and logos in `public/images/` (logos for the three ranges, cheese photos, flock, FEADER plaque).
- Flock: **2,200 Manchega sheep** (confirmed by the owner, 2026-10-04).
- FERCAMP Gold Medals (1990s): confirmed as real by the owner.
- WCA 2016 award: the medal (Silver) is confirmed. **Not confirmed:** category, score, diploma. Do not make these up.
- Allergens, shipping zones, delivery times, shipping costs and prices: **pending from the client.** Do not make these up.
- There are no real testimonials apart from those already in the code. Do not add new ones.

## Product Principles

1. Every page leads to a direct conversation (WhatsApp or phone).
2. Origin is the argument: own flock, raw milk, DOP, award. Prove it with facts, not adjectives.
3. Close and family-run, never like an industrial brand.
4. No claim without confirmation from the dairy.
